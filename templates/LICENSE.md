## Obligations for PreLegal

CC BY 4.0 permits copying, adapting, and commercial use, provided that we:

1. **Give appropriate credit** to Common Paper as the original author.
2. **Link to the license** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
3. **Indicate if changes were made** to the original.
4. Do not suggest that Common Paper endorses PreLegal or its users.

Documents that PreLegal generates from these templates for clients are adaptations and
must carry an attribution notice, for example:

> This document is based on the Common Paper [Agreement Name] (Version X.Y), available at
> [standardUrl], used under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
> It has been modified from the original.

The `name`, `version`, and `standardUrl` fields in `templates.json` provide the values
for this notice.

Common Paper templates are provided as-is and do not constitute legal advice.
